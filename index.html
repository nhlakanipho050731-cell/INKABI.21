How to interpret the evidence

Falling US real yields and a weaker dollar can support gold; rising real yields and a stronger dollar can pressure it. These are tendencies, not rules.

Risk events, central-bank guidance, positioning and price action can override simple correlations. Conflicting signals should result in NO-GO.
